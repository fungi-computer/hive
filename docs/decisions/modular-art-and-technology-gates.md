# Modular character art and technology gates

**Status:** direct Levi direction, 2026-09-07. This note preserves two future
seams; it does not authorize work in the current controls/home slice.

## Accepted direction: modular local character assembly

Before expanding the elf, dwarf, troll, or other mythic-art pack, establish one
authored local attachment recipe: a body/rig profile supplies named local frames;
an appearance recipe references compatible head, hair, garment, palette and
cosmetic parts; a pose moves those frames; equipped/held item identities remain
with the eventual inventory owner. Hair, hats, faces, packs and palm-held tools
attach to their relevant local frames. Unsupported fits are explicit authoring
incompatibilities, never silent human fallbacks or a universal scale multiplier.

Keep the existing Three geometry → fixed low-resolution bake → Pixi presentation
path. Bake complete posed appearances only when a real scene/study requests
them, reuse an identical assembled recipe, and invalidate the changed recipe on
an appearance/equipment change. Do not pre-bake the part Cartesian product or
add a skeleton/avatar/2D layering framework. The first proof is one biped with
interchangeable heads/hair/outfits, removable hat and palm-mounted tool, then one
short/broad fitted profile, inspected at native/game scale in the actual facings
and poses before authoring more mythic art.

## Accepted direction: technology is a real gate

Technology tiers are hard advancement gates, not labels over an unrestricted
recipe graph. A deliberate breakthrough opens eligibility for the next tier;
holding a higher-tier book, recipe, or finished item alone does not bypass it.
Within and across tiers, authored technique/recipe/facility dependencies remain
possible.

Keep four facts distinct: a learned technique, a trained practitioner's practical
skill, available workspace/tool/material prerequisites, and tier breakthrough
eligibility. A production attempt must explain which required fact is missing.
Losing a facility removes production capability without erasing knowledge, and
buying a finished advanced item need not grant its manufacturing technique.

Tier scope is deliberately open: a tier may ultimately belong to an individual,
colony, faction, or a named technical tradition. Nature, metalworking, husbandry,
alchemy, machinery and occult practice may progress differently; no single
civilization score or global unlock flag is selected. The first future proof must
exercise both gates—one visible breakthrough requirement and one specific learned
technique plus practical/workspace/material requirements—without adding a second
research engine.

## Boundary

These are accepted direction, not a current tech tree, character generator,
equipment system, runtime framework, or performance claim. Exact tier graph,
breakthrough requirements, era labels, knowledge sharing, balance and future
implementation owners remain proposals until a concrete caller is selected.
