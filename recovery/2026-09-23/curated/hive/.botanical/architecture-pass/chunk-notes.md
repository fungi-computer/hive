## Current caller-to-result path

`main.js:request` queues an ordered build/chop command. `clearing.js:step` accepts it on a fixed tick; `jobs.js:buildActivities` computes real pile pickup and delivery routes; `assignWork` offers those activities to libcolony. `advanceWork` walks first, and `transferWood` decrements the pile and puts that amount in `pawn.carry`; delivery moves it into `site.delivered`. `resources.js:dropCarried` moves carried wood back onto the ground when work is canceled or blocked. `clearing.test.js:conserved` proves piles + carry + delivered = felled × 6. Keep that chain and generalize its actor/cargo destination; a travel counter must not manufacture cargo or replace ownership transitions.

`world.js:inside` limits all routing to the 15×15 clearing; `movement.js:route` relies on that finite domain for termination. `construction.js:indoors` floods from that same clearing's physical edge. `art/scale.js:project` subtracts its center; `view.js` paints one whole clearing texture and constructs trees only from initial state. These are the concrete mismatches with streaming.

## Recommended seams

1. **Coordinates are stable; chunks are an index.** Retain integer `{x,z,level}` positions, `neighbors`, and cell keys within a named world coordinate space. Keep level 0 as current supported navigation; do not imply ramps/multiple floors now. Store `worldId` at the owning world and in cross-world locators; do not repeat a new identity on every local cell. Extract the authored 15×15 home extent from chunk width. A private initial chunk width such as 16 is a tunable storage/render choice, not a homeland border. `cx = floor(x/N)`, `lx = x-cx*N`, and likewise z; never truncate or use raw negative remainder. At N=16, −1 maps to chunk −1/local 15 and −16 to chunk −1/local 0. The same world-space actor ID and cargo remain unchanged when membership changes.

2. **World data answers availability and walkability.** Replace the finite-domain check at actual consumers with an explicit bounded world query. `blockedCells` currently reads `ROCKS`/`WATCHER` constants while placement reads `state.rocks`/`state.watcher`; a newly loaded rock can therefore block building while permitting walking. Move both through the same actual data owner. Keep arrays of trees/sites/piles inside chunks and ordered jobs as arrays. A chunk-key Map is justified by load/evict and nearby queries. A rebuilt cell occupancy Map/Set is justified by routing, placement, `wallMask`, and room neighbors. It is derived from authoritative records, never a second persisted truth. An entity lookup index is useful for existing target-by-ID callers once their arrays are partitioned; no generic ECS, archetype system, or global scans of every explored chunk are needed.

3. **Routes cross chunk edges and remain finite computations.** Keep cell paths and `walk` interpolation. Introduce a route result that distinguishes ready, no route in the searched domain, missing required data, and exhausted search budget. The present `null` cannot represent all four: `jobs.js:carryingOptions` treats it as a reason to drop cargo. Preload/pin a bounded route corridor ahead of a party, search across its chunk edges with the existing BFS first, and stop expansion at a configured node/chunk budget. Waiting for data holds the actor at its last valid cell with its cargo and intent intact. Revalidate each next cell as today. A long journey can request bounded successive segments; an entire infinite-world path or portal graph is unnecessary for this proof. Distinguish a failed segment search from proving no route exists anywhere.

4. **Persistence owns mutations; generation supplies only absent base data.** Generate deterministic base terrain from world seed, generator version, and global cell/feature coordinates. Use canonical feature ownership and neighbor sampling/halos for features spanning borders; neighboring chunk request order must not consume a shared RNG stream differently. Persist changed tree work/felled state, piles, sites and delivered material, actor identities/positions/inventory, party membership/intent, active jobs/claims, tick, ID allocation, and feed/routine state. Stable generated IDs can incorporate world/feature coordinates; dynamic IDs remain world-owned. A missing chunk save can use generation; an existing save must restore its mutations/tombstones. Never regenerate felled trees or missing piles on revisiting.

   At a coherent tick checkpoint, commit changed chunks and actor/cargo ownership together through the chosen store's ordinary atomic primitive, then permit clean unpinned data eviction. A failed write retains dirty data and prevents its eviction. For the first proof, persist bounded in-progress task/path/leg state rather than silently losing travel time or active deliveries on reload; rebuild indexes and render objects. A snapshot plus bounded command tail can replace unlimited retention of `state.commands`. This is a persistence contract, not a request to build custom transaction/log machinery or select a provider here.

5. **Three independent lifecycles.** Render residency means visible objects plus a sprite-overhang margin; it may disappear when the camera moves. Data residency means decoded chunk records needed by actors, jobs, navigation, construction analysis, or rendering; dirty/uncommitted data cannot disappear. Simulation activity means actors and work regions advanced by the one fixed-tick simulation owner, independently of camera visibility. Home work and a remote party remain active together. A dormant unoccupied chunk can have durable data without active simulation. Keep `lastSimTick` if useful for a later explicit catch-up policy, but do not derive elapsed work or resource replenishment from camera return. Bound render cache, data cache, loading queue/concurrency, path search, and retained command history; pins must be released with their owning activity. No render chunk implies a Durable Object, server, or independent clock.

6. **Camera/view is a projection, not a state mutation.** Separate fixed art-bake camera/anchors from a movable view center. Project global positions relative to a nearby camera origin using the accepted 32×16 ground diamond and vertical scale; invert that same view transform for input. Pixel-snap at display output, keeping actor/cell coordinates exact. This also avoids sending huge global coordinates into rendering math. Update all projection consumers together: pointer picking, placement ghosts/grid, routes/marks, characters, sites, cutaway, and the debug `project` consumer. Generate the placement grid only over the visible known region and give drag length its own product limit rather than reusing `SIZE`.

7. **Art stays original Three geometry → fixed low-resolution bake → Pixi.** The present `art/clearing.js:ground` is a cut-earth diorama with oversized backing boxes, rim plants, an authored trail, and rocks/marker baked into its single image. Repeating this 640×400 image makes physical seams and repeated obstacles. Preserve the home composition as authored terrain data, and bake seam-compatible ground tiles or small patches from the original geometry at the existing scale. Border halos/cropping must share the same global sampling and pixel alignment. Depth-bearing rocks/marker/foliage should be independently anchored sprites when they need to occlude actors; the ground layer cannot sort those objects against a person.

8. **Keep depth sorting global across visible chunks.** Current `view.js:put` uses x+z and one sortable `bodies` container. Preserve that shared sibling layer for visible trees, people, piles, walls, and other standing props, even if chunk membership owns their creation/culling. Installed Pixi 8.19.0 `lib/scene/container/container-mixins/sortMixin.mjs` calls `this.children.sort`; nested chunk containers cannot interleave each other's sprites. Use actual sprite bounds/overhang when deciding visibility so a tree rooted offscreen does not pop while its canopy is still visible. Deterministic equal-depth ordering is needed after a chunk reload. The roof `+50` in `construction-view.js:drawSites` is a finite-clearing shortcut, not a world layer guarantee; use an explicit roof/cutaway policy and verify nearby roofs and tall bodies visually. General multi-tile/isometric occlusion is not solved by increasing zIndex constants.

9. **Construction analysis crosses storage edges.** `indoors`, roof support, `shelteredBeds`, and `wallMask` query world neighbors, never chunk-local copies. For the proof, pin the complete home construction region plus a known exterior border and run the existing finite flood over that union, even if it spans chunks. A data-residency boundary is not evidence of outdoors. If a bounded analysis meets unknown continuation, report pending/unknown and fetch the needed region; do not change shelter based on camera position. Recompute derived room/occupancy information after relevant construction mutations; cache by construction revision when existing repeated `indoors` callers justify it. A full world room/portal graph is deferred until this bounded approach fails a concrete need.

## Smallest observable proof

Use one world/session simulation owner, two real people, existing libcolony assignment, and at least two adjacent chunks. Both initially cooperate at home. One receives a departure intent, acquires actual available wood through the same ownership rules, crosses the chunk boundary with persistent identity and inventory, then returns; the second continues a real home job while home is offscreen. Show progress and cargo at both ends, not just camera movement or a teleported marker. Crossing never performs an authority/server transfer.

Home data stays pinned because the worker needs it. To prove data eviction separately, visit and modify a third otherwise unoccupied wilderness chunk, leave it, commit and evict its data, then reload it and verify the stump/site/pile mutations. Restart from a saved coherent checkpoint to establish persistence beyond a render-cache demonstration. A later visit between named homelands is an explicit transfer edge between world owners; stable actor IDs, inventory, and named locations provide that future seam without implementing cross-owner handoff now.

Acceptance outcomes:

- Negative and positive coordinate boundaries round-trip; generating A then B produces the same shared border/features as B then A, without duplicate feature IDs.
- Identical commands/ticks give identical resource and work outcomes under different camera routes and render/data residency schedules when required simulation data remains pinned.
- Wood across saved chunks, ground piles, every person, and delivered/consumed construction balances through departure, blocked travel, cancellation, return, and reload. Extend the current conservation assertion rather than substituting counters.
- A path and a roofed room/door/bed straddling a chunk edge behave identically to the same arrangement inside a chunk. Missing data produces waiting/unknown, not fabricated blockage or outdoors.
- A camera sweep over a border has no ground gaps, duplicate decorations, tree-canopy popping, depth reversal, or incorrect picking; moving the camera away leaves the home worker progressing.
- Cache/queue/search limits and save failure have explicit bounded outcomes. A long outward-and-return route does not retain every explored render object or decoded chunk.
