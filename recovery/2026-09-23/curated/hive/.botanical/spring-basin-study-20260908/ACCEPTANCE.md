## Source/caller

`basin.js` exports `basin(parent, fill)` and `basinScene(fill, direction)` for `full | low | dry`. The same open lathed stone shell has real inner floor y=.055; low water sits at.165 and full at.285. Water is an opaque internal surface below the rim, not a cap on the basin body. Sediment is revealed when drained; the small rear fern marks direction. All geometry fits x/z±.465 within one cell; maximum height is.525. The existing112×112 camera(112,112,1.1), propAnchor, Three bake and Pixi silhouette picking are reused.

This low stone catch-basin depiction need not imply an endlessly flowing spring. No clock, stream, particle or fluid/gas simulation is implemented.
