## Pooling, batching, culling and original art

The current code already retains actor sprites and shares figure textures. Pixi's installed batcher can group compatible sprites; actual Hive draw calls have not been profiled in this audit. Current per-frame new hit rectangles and rebuilt graphics are concrete avoidable churn. Reuse durable display objects, update hit areas when their state changes, and limit world traversal to the visible set before adding pools everywhere.

A future arrow pool should reuse bounded storage/display slots, reset every launch's position/velocity/collision/trail fields and assign a fresh logical shot identity. Ammo consumption and impacts remain world transitions. Pooling does not replace draw batching or culling. Future sprite atlases should be generated from our original geometry with native-pixel/anchor/motion parity; they can reduce startup baking and texture switches. Measure boot, allocation/GC, draw calls, frame/tick time and cache residency separately. No projectile code or current 100-person performance claim came from this pass.
