## Translate into original game direction

- Goblin warrens feel crowded, damp, noisy, and exuberantly dirty: dubious communal stews, leftovers, sludge, spilled drink, messy feasts, and bodily gross-out comedy.
- Preserve the accepted cute silhouettes, expressive faces, and charming behavior. Favor comic mess and mischievous hospitality in the visible art and animation.
- A goblin sincerely offering a horrifying meal can be a good host. A welcoming goblin inn, guide, cook, or chess opponent can have different expectations from a human guest. The original bed-and-breakfast premise benefits directly from these competing ideas of comfort and hospitality.
- Keep individual characters and factions varied. A fastidious goblin brewer or a fussy guest still belongs; content definitions/preferences should express the differences.
- Where later systems support it, food preference, tolerated smells/mess, etiquette, comfort, and faction relations can create interactions. Physical waste, spills, decay, and treatment stay in the shared resource/environment systems. Flavor text does not remove their material or biological consequences.
- Original proposals to explore later: an unruly feast leaves cleanup work; a goblin guide knows a foul but useful path; hospitality improves relations even when the served dish horrifies another guest. These are hypotheses for small playable outcomes, not shipped mechanics.

## Levi's explicit inn-to-garden loop

Levi then connected goblin urine, feces, vomit, and inn cleaning directly to wastewater, compost, plant growth, and feeding guests without killing them. Record this as a desired interlocking hospitality outcome: food/drink → guests and waste → collection/cleaning → appropriate treatment and nutrient recovery → growing more food. The comic goblin culture gives the physical systems a reason to matter.

Keep water, solids, nutrient content, and contamination as distinct facts through their existing planned owners. Collection moves a problem; cutting/pumping transports it; treatment takes time and suitable conditions. Finished compost and treated water are separate outputs, and neither is an unconditional safe-food flag. Goblin enthusiasm for mess does not imply immunity to every hazard. Any tolerances belong to explicit character/species properties. Inputs, losses, storage capacity, and remaining residues keep this from becoming an infinite food machine.

The desired play is improving the inn's layout and processes—latrines, collection routes, treatment beds, compost and gardens—so hospitality scales without repetitive manual cleanup dominating. Individual cleaning and hauling use shared ordinary jobs and Work preferences. A future bounded proof should follow one identifiable waste batch through collection and treatment into a useful growing input, with finite accounting and visible failure/waiting reasons. This records the intended loop, not a request to add all its systems to the current candidate.

Delivery should associate the direction with existing creature/art #2, faction/hospitality #15, and relevant hygiene/food planning. Preserve upstairs → brewing and all current original-art custody. No new quote asset, voice recording, character implementation, or separate approval gate is requested.
