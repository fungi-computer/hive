# Gear, material processing, and knowledge: bounded mod discovery

Source-only discovery on 2026-09-07. No implementation, dependency adoption, experiments, or copied game assets/code. These are references for original Hive design. Read the current `ARCHITECTURE.md` / `PROTOTYPE.md` next-round inventory direction and `src/resources.js`: the existing material invariant is one physical owner across ground, hands, and work inputs. This discovery does not delay or expand the controls interim.

## Five useful design lessons

1. **The paper doll observes item ownership and compatibility.** RPG Style Inventory's [gear tab](https://github.com/SandyTheGreat/RPG-Style-Inventory/blob/acd1249309a00de16386936d805894c857b009f3/Source_1.6/Sandy_Detailed_RPG_Inventory/Sandy_Detailed_RPG_Inventory/RPG_GearTab.cs#L666) lists `pawn.inventory.innerContainer`, while apparel/equipment come from their existing holders; its [drop action](https://github.com/SandyTheGreat/RPG-Style-Inventory/blob/acd1249309a00de16386936d805894c857b009f3/Source_1.6/Sandy_Detailed_RPG_Inventory/Sandy_Detailed_RPG_Inventory/RPG_GearTab.cs#L1113) delegates to those owners. Hive's requested head/body/gloves/boots/back/belt display should similarly issue equip/unequip requests. Equipped gloves are apparel; held tools and hauling arms are activity occupancy, so a glove slot must not consume the person's ability to carry wood. A two-handed log and a wielded bow conflict because of hand use, while a worn pack and gloves may remain equipped.

2. **Capacity, packing, and current hand cargo are different constraints.** CE's [backpack definition](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Defs/ThingDefs_Misc/Apparel_Carrying.xml#L117) supplies a carrying-bulk bonus, a crafting recipe/material requirement, and a distinct backpack layer; its vest supplies capacity and an access/reload advantage at a worn-bulk cost. [CompInventory](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Comps/CompInventory.cs#L218) sums gear and inventory and checks mass/bulk limits. Hive's requested rotatable item grids and separate backpack/belt contents go beyond this inspected model: make placement belong to the actual bag item, and weight belong to the carried total. A backpack adds space, not unlimited lifting strength; a belt can offer small accessible storage without admitting a log. Dropping/transferring a filled bag moves the bag and its contents together; it must not turn its contents into unowned overflow. Start with the requested pack and belt, not arbitrary nested bags or a general container framework.

   Pick Up And Haul reinforces the distinction between supplies a person intends to keep and materials being delivered. Its [persistent hauled-item set](https://github.com/Mehni/PickUpAndHaul/blob/09f1fac96e70cc2a93d647615a5abd37734a3eba/Source/PickUpAndHaul/CompHauledToInventory.cs#L3) tracks hauling purpose, and its [unload driver](https://github.com/Mehni/PickUpAndHaul/blob/09f1fac96e70cc2a93d647615a5abd37734a3eba/Source/PickUpAndHaul/JobDriver_UnloadYourHauledInventory.cs#L81) transfers from inventory to hands before placement. Keep Hive's destination claims separate from physical location: canceled hauling must not drop a personal book, meal, or bow. The mod's later handling of merged item references is a reminder to preserve quantity/claim identity deliberately when stacks merge, not to adopt another duplicate inventory ledger.

3. **Primitive and refined processing should yield meaningful alternatives.** Medieval Overhaul's [rawhide and tanning racks](https://github.com/SirLalaPyon/MedievalOverhaul/blob/9bee1267304959a91940c0930ec61be268079992/1.5/Defs/ThingDefs_Buildings/Buildings_Processors.xml#L287) are distinct: drying produces untanned rawhide; the tanning rack uses tanning liquor and a knowledge prerequisite. Its [process definitions](https://github.com/SirLalaPyon/MedievalOverhaul/blob/9bee1267304959a91940c0930ec61be268079992/1.5/Defs/ProcessorDef/ProcessDef_Hides.xml#L18) define elapsed process time and weather/temperature factors. Its [liquor recipes](https://github.com/SirLalaPyon/MedievalOverhaul/blob/9bee1267304959a91940c0930ec61be268079992/1.5/Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_LeatherProduction.xml#L3) consume physical ingredients and work. The old direct hide-treatment recipes later in that file are commented out; do not mistake them for the active process.

   For Hive, choose one simple preparation/drying method and one learned tanning method with actual material benefits. Distinguish active labor from a batch waiting to cure. An unfinished batch owns its hide/reagent/work state across interruption and unloading; output appears once when completed and collected. The [product-extraction patch](https://github.com/SirLalaPyon/MedievalOverhaul/blob/9bee1267304959a91940c0930ec61be268079992/1.5/Source/MedievalOverhaul/Medieval%20Overhaul/Harmony%20Patches/CompProcessor_TakeOutProduct.cs#L73) concretely consumes ingredients and removes the active process after producing the material. Keep that conservation idea; do not copy its RimWorld butchery/Harmony/Processor Framework choreography, giant species list, timing constants, or full weather model. Rawhide and tanned leather need visibly useful differences before adding more processing steps.

4. **A book copy, a person's knowledge, and available recipes are separate facts.** Human Resources' [documentation job](https://github.com/jptrrs/HumanResources/blob/4cb8f9d9f33c83531b9088424e16c4322253623e/Source/Work/JobDriver_DocumentTech.cs#L22) selects a topic the author knows, performs recipe work, and produces a physical object for storage. The [bookshelf](https://github.com/jptrrs/HumanResources/blob/4cb8f9d9f33c83531b9088424e16c4322253623e/Source/Books%2BBuildings/Building_BookStore.cs#L61) owns books, tracks archive availability, and drops stored contents when removed. [Learning](https://github.com/jptrrs/HumanResources/blob/4cb8f9d9f33c83531b9088424e16c4322253623e/Source/Work/JobDriver_LearnTech.cs#L62) advances actor expertise; [CompKnowledge](https://github.com/jptrrs/HumanResources/blob/4cb8f9d9f33c83531b9088424e16c4322253623e/Source/Knowledge/CompKnowledge.cs#L353) records the learned topic independently and serializes expertise.

   Adopt the distinction, not its global library-unlock machinery. A courier can physically carry, lend, sell, or lose a tanning booklet. A recipient must study it through real work to learn the technique; afterward selling the copy does not erase learned knowledge. Removing the only available copy can prevent another person from studying it without undoing the first person's skill. Later copying a known technique into another book spends materials/time and creates another physical copy; it does not transfer or duplicate an actor record. Books are valuable caravan cargo, while expertise stays with its person.

5. **Choose gear recipes that close the travel loop before adding a technology tree.** CE's [carrying-gear base](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Defs/ThingDefs_Misc/Apparel_Carrying.xml#L26) connects craft work, material categories, unfinished product, and useful finished capacity. For Hive, a worn pouch/pack should visibly change what can be packed and carried on the next journey. Keep a small authored recipe list with explicit prerequisites; an individual worker either knows the method and has tools/materials/work access or the order explains what is missing. libcolony still assigns ready work; processing and knowledge do not install another scheduler. Begin with one crude and one refined material/gear result, not a generic recipe DSL, research-tree framework, broad item-affix system, or exhaustive medieval economy.

## Smallest playable later cut

First prove the equipment edge with one craftable crude pack, one belt pouch, and a paper doll containing back/belt plus the existing clothing/hand presentation. Each worn storage item exposes its own small grid; rotate an item to fit, carry a log visibly in the arms at the same time, and drop/re-equip the filled bag without losing or copying its contents. The belt need only demonstrate a distinct small storage area; fast-access balance can follow actual use.

Then make a single knowledge-and-crafting trip: one person brings a physical tanning booklet and a finite hide supply to another. The recipient receives and studies the booklet, prepares a simple tanning reagent, starts a hide batch, returns to ordinary work while it cures, and finishes a refined leather pack. That pack has one deliberate benefit over the crude version—capacity or durability, chosen visibly—then carries the booklet on the next trip while the person's arms haul wood. Initial hides/book can be supplied by a visitor; hunting, animal processing, paper manufacturing, combat, and multiplayer trading are not prerequisites for proving this loop.

Observe conservation through canceled work, claimed materials, bag transfer, chunk crossing, and save/reload. Verify that taking the booklet away stops an uncompleted reading assignment without erasing a learned technique; an untrained second person cannot craft the refined recipe merely because the first learned it. Processing cannot finish twice after reload. Full geometric packing and weight are independent checks, and over-capacity gear removal never destroys cargo. These are later bounded cuts; none belongs in the current two-person controls release.
