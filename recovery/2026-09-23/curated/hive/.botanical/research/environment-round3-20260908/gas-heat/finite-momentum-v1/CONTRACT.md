# First executable finite momentum shape

Status: source written, **not run**. Root first-shape review is required before the numerical qualification. PROPOSAL.md remains the original physical decision; this contract pins its approved source corrections and first experiment. No prior solver/evidence is modified.

## Source ownership and invariants

`finite-gas.mjs` evolves the existing thermal owner: cell mass, internal energy, one clock, source ledger and checked definition remain canonical. It adds active-face integrated momentum and cumulative component impulse references. Velocity and dual mass are derived. The former zero-provisional-velocity construction is removed. Each Euler thermal update computes one signed mass/enthalpy face receipt; `momentum.mjs` consumes that exact mass transfer to build dual fluxes and advect momentum. Both candidate pressure corrections use the single copied, unchanged `projection.mjs`. The Boussinesq velocity-advection/temperature-buoyancy caller and fixed-reference scalar transport are not imported into this candidate.

`momentum.mjs` owns the immutable derived component partitions, half-cell weights, canonical periodic interfaces, dual mass identity, upwind momentum exchange, actual body impulse, fixed-boundary reaction, velocity derivation and projection-to-momentum conversion. Its public layout consists only of deeply frozen plain geometry metadata; it exposes no mutable physical arrays. Geometry still comes from the private canonical-descriptor owner. Wall half-volumes are partitions used to constrain a component; they never add gas mass to the physical ledger.

Full-box geometry only: all cells fluid; no internal walls/obstacles/open sides. Outer walls are stationary, impermeable and inviscid free slip. Their normal half-volume momentum is constrained to zero and the removed impulse is recorded as wall reaction. Periodic seams are true paired interfaces. Gravity along a periodic axis rejects. No no-slip viscosity, reservoirs, digging/remapping or water displacement.

Fixed heater W and gravity are copied, validated and bound into the candidate identity. This is a temporary constant-source method limit, not future scene truth. An external body-force provider is captured by function reference and immutable version/definition identity. A resumed state bound to another provider identity rejects; callbacks are not serialized. The checked independent oracle is one such pure provider. Arbitrary hidden mutable/random callback state is not certified by an identity string.

Initial velocity must satisfy the known fixed-source divergence; zero is accepted only when compatible. The heating regression supplies the independent analytic initial velocity. No automatic initialization impulse, source-switch projection or clock-free velocity reset is introduced. All committed states validate this known constraint on read/save/restore, finite cell stocks, momentum shape and cumulative impulse balance.

Each Euler stage transports using the current actual velocity; candidate mass/U determine new dual mass and pressure target. Actual advected/forced momentum supplies `u_star`. The shared projection returns an impulse with the new density coefficient. Its scratch-backed velocity and pressure arrays are copied immediately. SSPRK2 averages mass/U/P and historical impulses, then performs a final endpoint constraint projection. Stage pressure impulses receive weights1/2,1/2, followed by final correction weight1. Aggregate face impulses and the last step's complete phase receipts are retained. Each Euler pressure receipt identifies force-evaluation time, constraint time and trial interval; the final correction is explicitly not labeled an endpoint pressure measurement.

The 200–600K thermal definition and approximate energy model remain. Physical U gains only prescribed heat and paired enthalpy transfers; pressure/kinetic residuals never reset U. Per-operation advection/body/wall/pressure kinetic changes, RK averaging kinetic change and mass-derived gravitational PE change are reported separately. Their finite bookkeeping is not exact thermodynamic U+K+PE conservation. No dynamic pressure, dual density or alternate thermal state is saved as physical truth.

## Independent checks and declared thresholds

1. For **each basis primal face**, independently construct physical cell net outflow, then compute the expected half-cell dual net outflow from signed coordinates and periodic wrapping. Compare it with the divergence of `dualTransfers`. Include both boundary half-volumes and every periodic mask. Coefficients are binary halves; require exact equality for unit basis vectors, correct dual physical volumes/centers and exactly one active face owner. This checks the linear map, not just a sine profile or total cancellation.
2. For dynamic dual mass closure retain `1e-12kg + 5e-13*oldDualMass`; body/pressure/wall momentum totals retain `1e-10kgm/s + 5e-12*sum(abs(reference and cumulative impulses))` per component. Physical mass/U retain prior limits: mass1e-12kg+2e-13*initialMass, U1e-8J+2e-13*max(initialU,abs(heat)), local EOS relative5e-10. One shared projection keeps all-cell integrated flux residual1e-10m³/s and linear residual1e-13.
3. Translation: velocity deviation <=1e-9m/s, periodic total momentum to the stated impulse tolerance, mass/U conservation, nonzero actual transport, exact cell-average translated sine reference. Require finer relative L1 mass error below coarse and finest error<2%; report actual diffusion and all component errors.
4. Both rest cases: maximum velocity <=1e-9m/s, mass/U differences within existing quantity tolerances, pressure **differences** at last Euler stages versus analytic column pressure. Compare phi/actual stage dt to the named static reference after gauge removal, with absolute pressure error1e-6Pa+relative1e-8 of hydrostatic range. Report pressure/body impulse cancellation and fixed-wall reaction; no isolated-fluid momentum conservation claim against walls.
5. Manufactured flow: compare all active face momenta and velocities with independent dual averages. At shared dt1/32 require coarse relative L2 velocity error<15%, fine<10% and fine/coarse<0.9. At the fine grid report all three errors and successive-solution differences; require the ratio of dt1/8→1/16 and1/16→1/32 L2 differences in[2.5,5.5] before claiming coupled second-order time behavior. A failed ratio is a method failure to review, not a reason to relabel temporal accuracy.
6. Manufactured pressure: retain raw copied impulses, force/constraint times and RK weights. Compare each last-step Euler phi/dt, after gauge removal, with the continuous **cell-volume average** pressure at its force-evaluation time; report first/second separately. This is a truncation diagnostic with candidate stage conventions, not an assertion that phi/dt equals instantaneous endpoint pressure. Proposed fine relative L2 threshold50%. The final endpoint constraint correction remains an impulse and is not folded into a fabricated endpoint pressure. Report spatial pressure errors and final correction magnitude.
7. Thermal regression: global p120kPa and heat432000J; exact cell-average density oracle and far-cold material law as in the frozen reference, with full-profile relative L1<2%, far-cold relative error<2e-6 for dt4. Report comparison with prior field values, rather than claim bit equality after adding momentum. Every local enthalpy receipt still reconstructs stocks without an EOS reset.
8. Mechanical operation receipts reconstruct total deltaK within1e-10J+1e-10*max(abs(Kstart),abs(Kend),sum(abs(recorded kinetic changes))). Independently reconstruct deltaPE from the primal mass receipts at the fixed gravity potential. Also report physical K+PE drift and omitted pressure-work/numerical dissipation; telescoping operation receipts alone do not validate physical mechanical conservation.
9. Exact same model/geometry/provider file reload and continuation; foreign provider identity, geometry or method rejects. Rejected request preserves full input including P and historical impulse references. Exercise actual CFL or envelope reduction, forbidden source/geometry changes, finite coefficient admission and budget exhaustion. All pressure phase arrays must remain unchanged after later projection calls.
