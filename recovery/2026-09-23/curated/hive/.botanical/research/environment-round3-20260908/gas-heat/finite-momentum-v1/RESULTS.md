## Remaining physical boundary

This is a qualified **small rectangular finite-momentum reference** with sealed free-slip or periodic faces, a single inert constant-gamma gas and fixed checked heater/gravity/force identity. Variable-density translation, stationary stratification, a constant-density nonlinear manufactured velocity and a1-D thermal expansion loop each pass. They do not yet qualify a moving3-D buoyant density front, an obstacle room/cave, no-slip walls, finite apertures/open reservoirs, viscosity, diffusion, composition transport, changing sources, topology edits, displacement, oxygen or combustion. The model does not conserve finite atmosphere under arbitrary excavation and never initializes gas after a terrain change.
