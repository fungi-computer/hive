# Coupling round-two acceptance, declared before execution

Owned research only. No production solver is selected. Python/Node standard libraries; no installed dependencies. Every executable proof uses the shared guarded run-proof wrapper.

1. A 1 m³ tank initially holds 0.2 m³ water at 293.15 K and 0.8 m³ dilute air at 303.15 K. An externally prescribed piston/inlet adds 0.25 m³ water at 283.15 K. With an explicitly open exhaust, a **new vented-displacement approximation** must export 0.25 m³ reference air, proportionate tracer and signed thermal anomaly; final gas volume is 0.55 m³. This is not an operation supported by either round-one or round-two fixed-volume gas solver. Unknown/closed exhaust rejects that approximation atomically.
2. Fixed-density displaced-air continuity is sum(outward carrier flow) = -dVgas/dt. Reusing the fixed-volume divergence-zero solve for this changing geometry is inadmissible. Water donor/receiver clipping must not silently alter gas carrier output.
3. Water and air retain separate thermal capacities. Later equilibrium is capacity-weighted; an unweighted temperature mean fails energy conservation. Negative anomaly is valid, not absent heat. No phase changes or calibration claim.
4. An independent ideal-gas counterexample keeps sealed gas mass and accounts positive compression work. At prescribed quasi-static adiabatic displacement, pressure and temperature rise. This analytic check explains why an unpressurized water solver cannot impose sealed displacement. A paired vented, constant-pressure/isothermal control-volume check uses enthalpy export and pressure work; using internal-energy export with the same work fails.
5. Atomic admission validates geometry/time/forcing compatibility, solver-specific successor and integrated exchanges; it never clips or rounds them. Rejected, stale and budget-interrupted candidates leave all committed state and history unchanged. A full checkpoint includes quantities, solver history, geometry revision, committed interval and forcing cursor.
6. Consume actual sibling physical solver implementations when stable. Same domain under 1/4/16 storage partitions and forward/reverse/shuffled discovery must preserve physical faces and complete state/history traces after canonical reconstruction. This is one-process storage partitioning, not independent partition solves or distributed-host proof. Restart at a committed substep must match uninterrupted continuation exactly on the tested runtime.
7. Halving dt compares physical approximation error separately from partition/restart equality. Splitting at a forcing boundary must match the equivalent explicitly subdivided interval; arbitrary dt subdivision is not expected to be bitwise identical. A geometry edit invalidates stale candidates and applies only at a shared committed boundary. Inertial water history must follow its solver's explicit remap policy.
8. Preserve failed runs. A passed accounting validator cannot establish ventilation benefit, water-wave accuracy, sparse-pressure locality, offline advancement or production population capacity. Sparse active work, pressure component dependency and known-forcing limits remain explicit.

Constants are illustrative: reference air density 1.2 kg/m³; air cp 1005 J/(kg K), cv 718 J/(kg K), R=287 J/(kg K); water density 1000 kg/m³ and cp 4180 J/(kg K). Ideal-gas examples compute mass from pV/(RT) rather than claiming reference density is a compatible equation of state.
