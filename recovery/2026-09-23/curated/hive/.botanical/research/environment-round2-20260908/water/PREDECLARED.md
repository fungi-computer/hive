# Round-two water experiment — preregistered before numerical runs

2026-09-08. SI units, authored numerical study only. Main 32×32 grid covers 64×64 m, 2 m square cells. Finite 12×16 m reservoir starts 0.6 m deep. A 4 m-wide gravity reach supplies a downstream withdrawal and a closed lateral diversion to a depression pond containing an island. Opening at 60 s, pond excavation at 180 s; observe 600 s. All outer boundaries are impermeable. Roughness n=0.07 s/m^(1/3). The gate is an open/closed horizontal connection, not a submerged sluice coefficient. Variant without diversion is the behavioral comparator. Physical geometry stays fixed when n=64 (1 m).

Candidate: staggered local-inertial update with theta=0.7 and transverse friction reconstruction. Solver-owned aggregate donor limiter is recorded; shared commit must only validate, never clip. Additional theta=1 comparison diagnoses numerical damping. Reference candidate: independently implemented first-order full shallow-water finite volume, Rusanov flux and hydrostatic reconstruction; it only earns reference status after rest/dry-dam-break checks. No code borrowed from game runtime. Both authored implementations may fail.

Thresholds (fixed; report failure, do not relax after seeing results):

* Closed-domain initial stock = final liquid + downstream collected, relative imbalance <=1e-10; minimum depth >=-1e-12 m. Resting uneven-bed lake maximum depth and discharge/momentum drift <=1e-11 in SI units over 120 s.
* Reference qualification: flat frictionless dry dam-break (Ritter), pre-boundary-interaction at t=2 s. Normalized integrated depth error <=8% at dx=0.5 m, decreases on dx=0.25 m; neither negative depth nor dropped water allowed. This does not qualify arbitrary 2D sharp-bed geometry.
* Useful diversion: by 600 s diverted pond gains >=10 m³ against gate-closed comparator, downstream collection is >=10% less, reservoir loses >=25% initial water. Closed diversion before 60 s permits <=1e-10 m³ transfer. Digging preserves liquid exactly within balance tolerance, and changes subsequent pond storage by >=1 m³ against no-dig comparison.
* dt refinement: compare max dt=0.1/0.05/0.025 s on 32². Pond volume and downstream collected differ <=5% between 0.05/0.025; 1 cm arrival time differs <=10% or 2 s (larger allowance). Spatial refinement 2→1 m: pond/collection differences <=15%, arrival <=20% or 4 s. Report genuine geometry and solution differences separately.
* LI vs qualified full SWE: pond and collection within 15%, arrival within 20% or 4 s. These are gameplay-study tolerances, not a hydraulic validation standard. Report all measured Froude distribution/steep gradients and time/depth weighting; good bulk agreement cannot authorize high-Froude breach/jet behavior. A source-front outside the approximation is a finding.
* 90° transformed fixture: mapped complete-state error <=1e-10 SI. Radial wetting front: axis/diagonal 1 cm arrival-radius difference <=10%, and decreases on 2→1 m refinement (report grid/threshold effects). No orientation claim from total mass alone.
* Reload with complete state/history: exact JSON numerical continuation in supported Node runtime. Deliberately discarded LI discharge must be detectably different (>1e-12); topology revision changes only its explicitly edited beds/faces and affected discharge. No capacity deletion.
* Each unsuccessful numerical run keeps its own result JSON/log and source hash. A successful executable exit is experiment completion, not automatic candidate acceptance.

No claimed Richards calibration or duplicated aquifer stock.
