Recommended contract:

1. **Paint storage onto usable floor.** Create, extend, shrink and name zones; configure accepted goods and destination priority. Zones own policy and membership, never copies of contents. Lots retain ground custody. Start with one storage-zone policy per cell and exclude blocked/unsupported cells and occupied storage/machine footprints. Reject overlapping zone ownership explicitly. Changing/removing a designation leaves physical goods untouched; newly disallowed goods become relocation candidates.
2. **Capacity belongs to the actual destination.** Extend the existing typed destination to ground cell or container. A floor transfer resolves one checked cell before reserving its incoming space; a zone ID is policy, not a physical destination. The materials owner evaluates occupied space plus incoming promises per cell, including loose goods already there. Use an explicit bounded compatible-stack/bulk policy; exact floor and crate limits need a product choice. Zone totals are derived sums, never pooled space permitting several deliveries onto one full cell. Rendering groups goods without merging lot identities. Policy edits wake ordinary demand/revalidation; the zone editor never moves lots itself. Automatic hauling remains intended, through the existing reservation/pickup/delivery/drop lifecycle.
3. **Use ordinary hauling and withdrawal.** Storage demand offers existing transfer work to libcolony, preserving personal orders, Work flags and carried continuation. Storage priority ranks destinations separately from job urgency. Automatic relocation promotes goods only to a strictly higher priority or fixes invalid placement; stable distance/ID ties prevent equal-priority churn. Extend source selection and pickup to accessible stored portions, preserving source-origin evidence and excluding destination/self, reserved portions and process-owned inputs. Stored wood must feed ordinary construction before storage is complete.
4. **Build a readable Dwarven progression.** Floor piles → timber/iron crates and labelled drawers → powered chain rollers, hoppers and mechanical pullers → filtered junctions and hoists. A useful first machine loop is crate → short visible conveyor → workshop input buffer, with manual loading/withdrawal available. Buffers request bounded target quantities; priority is separate from quantity limits. Waterwheel, crank or runic drives are possible content directions; their exact energy economics remain unsettled. Boxes and ports expose the same contents/capacity rules.
5. **Share custody, distinguish movement.** Workers walk; machinery advances on authoritative ticks with power and connectivity. Belts use bounded segment buffers with real lot custody and saved movement progress. During transit, one authoritative location identifies the segment/buffer holding the lot; progress refers to that same lot, never a second moving item alongside a ground/container copy. Add the smallest typed machine-transfer operation inside the existing goods owner when that consumer arrives; never impersonate a pawn or add a separate belt inventory/claims store. Atomically validate source, destination and capacity at each handoff. Animation interpolates the resulting state.
6. **Full means waiting.** Full/blocked output retains goods and backs up upstream; stop intake when its buffer is full. Power loss freezes motion without deleting stock. Policy edits invalidate affected promises through the same owner. Cancellation releases unpicked claims; picked goods remain held or safely placed once. Teardown preflights contents and both incoming/outbound claims. Save/reload preserves lots, reservations, buffers and progress, restores paused, and cannot replay a completed handoff.

Wake affected demand on goods/policy/topology changes using owner-derived indexes and bounded candidate work. The floor-stockpile proof designates floor, automatically stores permitted mixed goods, withdraws wood into construction, and preserves physical goods across zone removal and paused reload. A separate later machinery proof exercises one short powered crate/belt/buffer connection with last-space contention, blockage, power loss and paused reload. Check per-material conservation and unique custody throughout. These extend the [existing transfer laws](../../docs/decisions/unified-work-algebra-recut.md#required-transfer-laws); neither introduces a new scheduler or general automation framework, and the machine proof is not a floor-stockpile release gate.

Distinguish a preferred storage quota from a genuinely hard physical capacity rather than making a policy edit corrupt the save. A future belt also needs one explicit movement/time budget: iterating segment objects cannot move a lot through an arbitrary number of segments in one tick. Fixed transport progress, atomic custody changes and backpressure must survive reordering, power loss and reload.

Land floor stockpiles as a separate player-facing storage outcome after the current common-transfer checkpoint; evaluate its exact ordering with mixed shelves and brewing at that handoff. Dwarven conveyors, hoists and powered buffers remain later technology consumers, not requirements for the first brew. No current runtime or map expansion is added by this note.
