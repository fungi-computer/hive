# Darkest Dungeon: why it fits the home/expedition idea

Astra personally read the developer-authored 2015 account and Red Hook's
current original-game page, plus the GDC2016 session abstract. The GDC talk
itself was not watched. This is research for future direction, no game changes.

## What primary sources establish

Bourassa and Sigman frame the design around adventurers' responses to pressure.
Stress can change behavior; individual preferences affect recovery in town.
They deliberately made the stress meter legible and used contextual speech and
symbols to explain autonomous behavior. They describe personal quirks and
remembered patterns as ways to give a hero an unfolding history. This is their
2015 development account, not a claim that every number persists in current DLC.
[Developer account](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-darkest-dungeon-s-i-affliction-system).

Red Hook describes the original game as leading a team whose capabilities and
problems extend beyond fighting monsters. That supports a people-centered
expedition reference alongside Diablo's gear reference.
[Original-game page](https://www.darkestdungeon.com/darkest-dungeon/).

The GDC2016 abstract says assembling RPG, boardgame, roguelike and wargame
influences into a comprehensible whole was itself a design challenge. It is
evidence for the session's scope only, not watched-talk findings.
[GDC session](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1023435/Darkest-Dungeon-A-Design).

## Hive recommendations — inference

1. The same persistent person should work in the colony, join an expedition,
   return with possessions/experiences, and recover at home. Party membership,
   destination and activity change; identity and ownership do not get copied.
2. Make expedition preparation and returning worthwhile: choose people and
   supplies, explore, decide when to leave, bring home materials/knowledge,
   then build/craft/trade/recover. A bed, kitchen, garden, shop and familiar
   gain meaning through that loop. Colony workers may continue ordinary jobs
   when the expedition is away, under the common world clock/authority.
3. Explain consequential actor behavior visibly. A future fear, fatigue or
   loyalty rule needs a reason a player can inspect; character barks can add
   personality but cannot replace accurate UI or command results. Do not add
   hidden arbitrary command refusal to the current drafting/order correction.
4. Tune dangerous excursions and restorative home life together. Permanent
   losses, stress, retreat penalties and afflictions are design choices to test,
   not obligations inherited from Darkest Dungeon. Preserve Levi's cozy art
   and the viability of builders, crafters and shopkeepers who prefer home.

Levi explicitly connects party+dungeon+settlement to Darkest Dungeon. Procedural
goblin caves, returned construction/craft materials, RP shops and faction
goodwill are his future direction. The above recommendations specify a coherent
connection; they are not implemented systems or a separate simulation engine.
