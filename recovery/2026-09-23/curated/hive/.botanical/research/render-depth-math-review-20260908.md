# Depth relation math review

**The proposed separation rule is sound but incomplete under explicit preconditions.** Current `art/scale.js:17` yields a toward-eye vector proportional to `(12, 9.798, 12)`. Two points on the same orthographic camera ray differ by `t * eyeDirection`. Therefore, if all of A’s transformed geometry satisfies `A.maxX < B.minX`, any actual shared-ray B point is nearer than A. The same argument holds for Y or Z. One genuinely separating axis suffices; “greater anchor sum” and projected rectangle centers do not.

This requires envelopes containing the complete rendered geometry, current transforms and one consistent camera basis. Opposite separations on different axes mean the exact volumes cannot share a camera ray; do not choose whichever axis was checked first. Report that pair as non-overlapping/diagnostic uncertainty if padding or numerical error produced the candidate. A graph cycle across different pairs is a different problem: real pairwise constraints may require splitting renderables rather than discarding an edge.

## Corrections before claiming proof

1. **Tolerance must weaken conclusions.** Use `A.max < B.min - epsilon` for strict proof; classify the epsilon band as touch/uncertain. `A.max <= B.min + epsilon` can assert the wrong order. Counterexample: A is `[0,1]^3`; B occupies X `[0.999,2]`, Y `[-1,0]`, Z `[0,1]`. A is in front on overlapping rays by Y, but epsilon `0.01` applied to X incorrectly declares A behind. Separate numeric tolerance from one-pixel stylistic padding.

2. **A geometric hull is not the exact baked silhouette.** `art.js:13` adds a one-pixel outline; `scale.js:29` rounds world projection; portrait/prop cameras have different targets and anchors. Build screen candidates in the _actual texture-anchor plus sprite-position coordinate system_, with conservative raster/outline padding or registered alpha extents. A mathematically correct continuous hull test can otherwise omit visible pixel overlaps. Padding-only contact does not establish an actual 3D occlusion relation.

3. **Current tree rocking breaks translation-only equivalence.** `view.js:264` rotates the whole baked tree container in screen space while chopping. Updating only its screen candidate hull leaves the old world AABB unjustified for the rotated image. Either include an equivalent camera-basis transform in envelopes or explicitly limit the first proof to unrotated subjects and report rocking as pending integration. Likewise, capture each selected figure phase/facing before disposal; `figures.js:682/695` changes kneeling/sleep transforms substantially. Use fractional stair `visualPosition`, not rounded logical level.

4. **Contact has stage-specific meaning.** Finished `floor.js` has top Y=0 and joists below; finished `home.js:96 bed` begins at Y=0. At the same upper datum, a documented floor-before-supported-bed tie is appropriate. It is not a universal “floor first” rule: lower actors may lie behind the elevated floor, stakes extend above the plane, and roofs already contain local height. Do not enlarge epsilon to conceal pose penetration. Sleeping bodies have their own bed-contact offset; a walkable bed intersection is gameplay/contact debt, not a sorting theorem.

5. **Whole sprites remain a hard limit.** A stair has near and far rails around the occupant; one actor may need its torso in front of rear structure while its feet sit behind the foreground rail. An aggregate AABB cannot solve that, and a deterministic fallback cannot claim it did. Whole-mesh convex hulls are conservative; use explicit unresolved output to identify the smallest authored render-part split. Shadows, progress, nameplates and cutaway opacity need separate presentation rules.

## Weakest useful real-builder fixture

Use actual bed directions 0/1 plus Rowan around both sides/ends; one lower actor, upper floor and upper bed/actor; a stair actor at start/middle/landing; and tree/actor overlap. Include one true separated case, exact floor/bed touch, deliberate bed penetration, and rail ambiguity. Require insertion-order-independent constraints, not identical arbitrary tie ordering. Show both candidate outlines and reasons/unresolved pairs. This is sufficient to challenge the architecture before a full animation matrix; it is not actual-game acceptance.
