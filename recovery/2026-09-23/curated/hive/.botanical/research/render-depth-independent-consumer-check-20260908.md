# Independent render-depth consumer feasibility

**Feasible before the v7 handoff:** a new bounds/relation module plus an isolated fixture can exercise the actual original mesh builders without touching `art.js`, `view.js`, `construction-view.js`, main, or simulation. This is source feasibility, not a claim a fixture has been implemented or run.

The smallest useful consumer imports `building`/`wallJoint` from `art/home.js:109/129`, `tree` from `art/clearing.js:104`, and `figure` from `art/figures.js:670`. It constructs fresh real subjects, updates their world matrices, traverses meshes only, copies transformed geometry bounds into plain numeric local envelopes, then translates each envelope by `(x, level * STOREY_HEIGHT, z)`. Mesh transforms already include facing, limb animation and scale; do not rotate the resulting bounds again. Lights in `geometry.js:46 scene()` are not geometry.

A pure fixture needs no WebGL to check these envelopes and deterministic relations: bed in both directions with actor positions around its complete footprint; lower actor / upper floor / upper actor; stair approach, middle and landing; foreground trunk/canopy; changed wall masks. Projected overlap only identifies candidates. Emit proven ordering constraints plus explicit unresolved/cyclic cases; an AABB overlap does not prove an arbitrary total order. Stable ordering of unconstrained items is useful, but cannot repair genuinely interleaving geometry.

An optional browser fixture can take those **same scene instances**, record metadata, then call existing `art.js:26 bake` with existing `camera`/`anchor` and display the resulting textures using ordinary Pixi sprites. That reuses the current Three → low-resolution bake → Pixi path. It must not implement a replacement rasterizer, clone the game view, or regenerate metadata from guessed art dimensions. A local diagnostic entry need not enter Vite production inputs or main startup; served/deployed discovery is a later explicit integration.

## Later actual-game hooks and traps

- **Bake seam:** capture bounds inside `bake`, before its geometry-disposal traversal at `art.js:35`, and associate copied metadata with the returned texture. Every existing figure/prop/joint loop then supplies matching metadata automatically. `geometry.js:3` caches shared materials: dispose fixture-owned geometry/textures, not shared materials. No per-frame mesh reconstruction.
- **View seam:** replace `view.js:20 depthKey`/`:24 put` and `construction-view.js:178` scalar offsets with one owner ranking their shared `bodies` container. Construction selects the actual joint-mask/stage texture at line162; actors select actual pose/facing/frame at `view.js:468`. Bind depth metadata after those selections; otherwise placement can use yesterday’s pose bounds. Finish ranks after all render branches update, and preserve Pixi hit traversal against that same order.
- **Support is semantic:** `floor.js` boards finish at local `y=0`, with joists below; roof geometry is already around local `y=2.3`. Do not elevate roofs twice or treat every level-1 object as a surface. Stair supplies its landing; ordering metadata cannot create another floor or change support/collision. Bed extends from local `z=-0.44` to `1.44`, and ordinary walkers may intersect it: sorting cannot fix that contact policy.
- **Motion:** use `movement.js:visualPosition` including fractional x/z/**level** during the 18-tick stair traversal. Logical actor level and rounded visibility selection are not the render translation. Whole stair/tree envelopes can remain ambiguous; exposed interleaving may require render parts, not larger offsets.
- **Presentation:** `view.js:30 body` includes a separate Pixi shadow, selection ring and progress; nameplates live on `app.stage`. Those are not mesh bounds or occluders. Ground/route/ghost/debug/night layers and cutaway visibility retain explicit presentation roles. Alpha hit silhouettes remain the existing picking owner’s responsibility, distinct from volume bounds.

Exit: a runnable isolated relation/optional pixel diagnostic with real builders is useful independent progress. Final acceptance still requires the natural v7 checkpoint and actual bake/view integration; it cannot be claimed from synthetic envelopes alone.
