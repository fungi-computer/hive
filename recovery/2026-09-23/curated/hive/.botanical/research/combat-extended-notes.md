## Actual shooting path

`Verb_ShootCE.TryCastShot` prepares available ammo, calls the projectile-launch implementation, and notifies ammo consumption after a successful cast. `Verb_LaunchProjectileCE.TryCastShot` creates a projectile, calls `ShiftTarget` to calculate launch angles, and launches with source location, angle, rotation, speed, and height. `ProjectileCE.Tick` advances the projectile's simulation position and checks the segment from its previous position before applying impact.

### Aim becomes a launch condition

[`Verb_LaunchProjectileCE.ShiftTarget`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Verbs/Verb_LaunchProjectileCE.cs#L510) distinguishes target-location/visibility offset, estimated distance, moving-target lead, sway/recoil, target height, and mechanical angular spread. [`ShiftVecReport.GetRandSpreadVec`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/ShiftVecReport.cs#L209) samples angular spread from a disk. The final launch uses horizontal rotation plus vertical angle. A comment calls one vector radians, but the producer documents degrees and the caller converts its vertical component; do not copy comments or unit conventions uncritically.

**Useful local adaptation:** one aim point, one bow speed, a windup, and a small seeded angular error sampled once at release. Persist the release parameters/seed as simulation facts. More aim time may later narrow spread, but range-estimation, recoil, suppression, weather, and lead models are unnecessary for one stationary target. The hit comes from subsequent flight and collision, not a hit-roll followed by decorative arrow animation.

### Flight is persistent, and movement happens on ticks

[`BaseTrajectoryWorker`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Projectiles/TrajectoryWorkers/BaseTrajectoryWorker.cs#L47) converts speed/launch angles to velocity and solves launch elevation from distance and height difference. [`LerpedTrajectoryWorker`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Projectiles/TrajectoryWorkers/LerpedTrajectoryWorker.cs#L43) computes horizontal travel and ballistic height from elapsed ticks. A separate [`BallisticsTrajectoryWorker`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Projectiles/TrajectoryWorkers/BallisticsTrajectoryWorker.cs#L25) changes velocity for acceleration, drag, and gravity. [`ProjectilePropertiesCE`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Projectiles/ProjectilePropertiesCE.cs#L93) chooses the worker; the property fallback is the lerped worker. This inspection did not resolve every inherited XML definition or optional patch into a runtime configuration, so it does not assert that every CE arrow uses one worker.

**Useful local adaptation:** a small active projectile record with stable ID, shooter ID, origin, launch velocity, launch tick, current/previous position, and terminal state. Use Hive's existing 50 ms simulation step. A simple gravity arc is sufficient; do not bring over drag, caliber, kinetic energy, rockets, or the extensible trajectory-worker hierarchy. Arrow height is continuous world/art height, with release elevation derived from storey base plus hand height; people can retain integer cell/storey positions. Rendering only observes and interpolates that flight. Bounds on flight lifetime/range and active projectiles prevent unbounded retained objects.

### Collision uses the travelled segment and physical bounds

[`ProjectileCE.CheckForCollisionBetween`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Projectiles/ProjectileCE.cs#L930) visits cells crossed between old and new positions. [`CanCollideWith`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Projectiles/ProjectileCE.cs#L1177) intersects a ray with bounds and limits acceptance to the current segment. [`CollisionVertical`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/CollisionVertical.cs#L30) derives vertical extents from RimWorld buildings, doors, plants, pawn posture, and nearby cover. CE still includes stochastic plant interception after bounds intersection; its model is not uniformly deterministic geometric collision.

**Useful local adaptation:** explicit authored target/body/cover volumes in world coordinates. Ground-cell occupancy is broad-phase candidate selection, not proof of a hit. Test the swept arrow segment against candidates and resolve the nearest physical impact, independent of intended target ID or sprite alpha. Include intervening cover and ground; when upper floors exist, their slabs and roofs have explicit collision height. No endpoint-only checks that let a fast arrow skip thin cover. Use a bounded subdivision only if the measured gravity-arc chord approximation needs it. Original geometry can inform simple collision dimensions, but rendering pixels and Pixi hit areas must not become simulation authority.

### Ammo is held, consumed, and sometimes recoverable

[`Verb_ShootCE.TryCastShot` / `OnCastSuccessful`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Verbs/Verb_ShootCE.cs#L393) bracket launch with ammo availability and successful-shot notification. [`CompAmmoUser`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Source/CombatExtended/CombatExtended/Comps/CompAmmoUser.cs#L418) handles magazine counts or an inventory stack item; the latter can be split before launch and is destroyed on success. [`Defs/Ammo/Neolithic/Arrows.xml`](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended/blob/c63c9656c0837371014b45519bfd6becfa0bc4ac/Defs/Ammo/Neolithic/Arrows.xml#L41) defines carried arrow stacks, projectile mappings, crafting recipes, and chance-based arrow spawning after impact.

**Useful local adaptation:** extend Hive's existing physical-resource ownership rule: available/carried arrow → released projectile → recoverable spent arrow or explicitly consumed/lost arrow. Commit consumption and projectile creation in one fixed-tick transition; canceled windup or rejected launch leaves inventory intact. The first proof can deterministically leave a spent arrow at impact, or mark it consumed; recovery odds and multiple ammo types are optional later. Do not copy CE's gun magazines, loadout automation, ammo taxonomy, spawn-via-explosion definitions, or preparation/notification choreography.

## Proposed first bow-and-target checkpoint

One original bow, one arrow type, one stationary practice target, and a movable/constructible solid cover obstacle. Reuse the current simulation/job/resource seams. A person obtains a finite set of actual arrows (an initial finite supply is sufficient; a new crafting system is not a prerequisite), receives a practice-shot order, walks to a valid firing cell, winds up, and releases exactly one. Libcolony continues to own assignment of ready pawn/activity work; a projectile update is an outcome of that work, not another optimizer or AI loop.

The shot records a sampled launch direction, follows a visible arc over multiple fixed ticks, and ends at target, cover, or ground. Zero-spread and miss cases show that geometry determines impact. The target records only a simple hit/result; no health, body-part, armor, faction, or combat AI framework. Bow/arrow/target visuals remain original Three geometry baked at the accepted low resolution into Pixi; render at true projected height with a ground shadow if useful.

Acceptance observations: a released arrow spends one inventory unit; no-ammo and canceled-windup attempts spend none; an obstacle between shooter and target receives the nearer hit; a fast arrow cannot skip thin cover between ticks; a miss lands visibly; identical release seed/commands/ticks give identical impacts at different render cadences and with the camera elsewhere. Active projectile flight pins the necessary bounded collision region across chunk edges; offscreen flight still progresses, and save/reload during flight cannot duplicate ammo or impact. A second-storey firing test can follow once that building/navigation checkpoint exists, without redefining storey indices as projectile height.
