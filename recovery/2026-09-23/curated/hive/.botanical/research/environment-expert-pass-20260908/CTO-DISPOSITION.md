### The shared architecture decision

Share **geometry identity, units, world time and accounted transfers**. Keep water momentum, air circulation and thermal conduction as separate physical laws. The common module must not become one generic rate equation or one bag of optional fields.

The scale fork's earlier generic “limit bundled quantities” pseudocode is superseded: each solver must supply an admissible successor state and integrated exchanges for the agreed geometry revision and interval. Shared accounting validates paired transfers and declared sources/sinks, then commits quantities, solver history and frontier together. It does not independently rescale or clamp solver outputs. Independent rounding of gas carrier faces can also break their divergence constraint.

Geometry reports solids, void volume, physical surfaces, material contacts and apertures at a revision. Actor navigation and build previews are separate queries: an unfinished wall blueprint need not obstruct air, and a stair route does not define hydraulic connectivity. Render cutaway and map LOD change none of these quantities. Explicit metre/geometry conversion and solver resolution are required; a logical storey is not a physical unit or sufficient resolution for breathing-height versus ceiling smoke.

Each physical stock and future-affecting solver history has one owner. Store liquid volume and the selected water method's face discharge; derive water level from volume and geometry. Store the thermal quantity appropriate to the chosen approximation; derive temperature. Persist changed fields, forcing cursor, geometry revision and committed time together. Never recreate a drained pond or erase a heated room when a map chunk leaves the camera.

All competing withdrawals must respect a donor's actual stock, but not every solver can be repaired by scaling its output edges. Incompressible air circulation must satisfy its divergence constraint; independent carrier-flow clipping would break that constraint. Shorten the shared substep or re-solve instead. Signed thermal anomaly can be negative and has no inventory-style positivity clamp. Conduction stability depends on the aggregate connected conductance/heat capacity, not just each pair. Conservation, numerical stability and useful physical behavior are separate acceptance requirements.

Water rising into air changes the available gas volume. That coupling requires one conservative displacement/pressure transaction. The first fixed-volume dilute-smoke approximation does not support flooding its air cells, oxygen depletion, sealed pressure, steam or realistic combustion. Those behaviors need explicit model/state extensions before gameplay claims. Likewise a slow surface-water approximation cannot quietly become the solver for violent dam breaks or pressure pipes.

Keep first experiments within a bounded physical fixture with explicit boundary conditions. Do not discover a connected atmosphere or watershed by flooding the entire planet. A changed unknown neighbor needs compatible state/history; it is not automatically an infinite sink or ambient-air source. Chunk borders cannot block or duplicate exchange. A sparse smoke plume also does not prove its pressure solve is local. Actual solver partition, refinement and work-budget measurements must precede scale claims.

### Next proof choices

1. **Surface water:** a finite source, gated/diggable diversion and pond with a downstream user. Compare local inertia against implicit diffusion and a well-balanced shallow-water reference where its faster flow requires one. Check wet/dry fronts, stable resting levels, orientation/refinement, edit conservation and exact supported-runtime reload including discharge. A pretty moving surface is insufficient.
2. **Atmosphere:** retain the small two-level fire/opening/vent objective, but compare source-local exhaust, vent height, replacement-air routes and resolved counterflow. Success must improve measured occupant exposure and clearing time while preserving balances; failure remains a finding, not a reason to lower the goal to a passing conservation assertion.
3. **Soil and scale follow their actual consumers:** shallow porous columns must demonstrate rain infiltration, well drawdown and reversed seepage without overlapping groundwater inventories. Then run the selected water/air solvers, including their history, under 1/4/16 partitions and restart/refinement. The current scalar toy cannot substitute for those tests.

These are next discriminating research outcomes. Production remains focused on the tiny home: unified goods/transfer, usable mixed storage and finite-input brewing, then one carried-water plant-establishment consumer. That first finite-water action can earn the liquid custody boundary without waiting for continental hydraulics. The studies support the long-term direction of ponds, irrigation, wells, greenhouses and useful environmental consequences; those systems are not yet playable on the preview.
