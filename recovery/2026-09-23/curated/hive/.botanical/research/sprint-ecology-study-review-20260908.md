The perennial and lifecycle notes are architecture/source audits: they document Hive's current mugwort, oak, goods, save, and fixed-clock behavior, then propose laws rather than reporting an implemented ecology.

## Shared mechanisms and boundaries

All useful directions compose through six narrow owners:

- geometry owns solids, elevation, openings, channels, work positions, and connection revisions;
- one quantity/custody owner debits sources and moves finite water, feedstock, waste, harvest, and substrate exactly once;
- an environmental owner, when earned, advances conserved heat and transported constituents; room summaries and visuals are projections;
- organisms query shared local conditions, while their typed process owners advance development, stress, reproduction, death, or fungal use;
- the authoritative clock and persistence owner settle changes in order, preserve cursors and identities, and rebuild caches;
- inspectors explain current amount, route, limiting condition, and next useful action.

These owners must not collapse distinct facts. Surface water, soil water, air, energy, nutrients, contamination, organism identity, physical material, and claims are separate. A channel changes a real connection; a particle or moisture texture changes none. Energy can cross a sealed exchanger without gas or water. Waste becomes compost, treatment output, or crop input only through a named finite process. A claim never becomes physical custody, and save/reload never repeats a transfer or lifecycle outcome.

The current source cited by the retained audits has no water quantity, soil reserve, heat/gas field, compost process, perennial development, provenance-bearing tree biomass, or ecology catch-up scheduler. Mugwort stage is elapsed-tick based; oaks produce anonymous wood through a tiny-demo shortcut. The tracked decisions label environmental and living-world mechanisms as future candidates, not shipped capabilities.

## Smallest useful first consumer

After the current work/material migration and brewing slice are coherent, the first ecology consumer should be **one carried-water establishment step for the existing mugwort bed**. Give one named local source a finite stock; ordinary work fills one physical vessel, carries it under existing claim/custody rules, and credits one bed reserve. The existing plant reads that reserve through a narrow condition query. A dry newly sown plant reports “needs water”; one successful delivery changes it to growing and debits the reserve. Keep this as an establishment action rather than a frequent chore until play feedback proves repeated watering fun.

The player-visible exit is: sow mugwort, designate watering, watch a worker fill/carry/apply water, see the reason change, then harvest the same plant into the brewing/material loop. Cancellation preserves the vessel and both balances; pause freezes movement; save/reload resumes with identical custody and progress. Focused proof should cover quantity conservation, unique location, idempotent completion, dry pause/recovery, deterministic continuation, and candidate/path cost for the actual tiny scene. This establishes no flowing-water or world-scale capacity claim.
