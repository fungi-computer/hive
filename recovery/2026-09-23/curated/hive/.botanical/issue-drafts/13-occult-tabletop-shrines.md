# World direction: occult chess, tarot, magic, shrines, and cults

Status: loose future world direction from Levi, not an implementation commitment or an expansion of the active two-person-home goal.

Null Tale is another explicit art/atmosphere reference, including [Chess of Death (currently titled Fate Chess)](https://nulltale.itch.io/chess-of-death) and the already reviewed [LutLight2D](https://github.com/NullTale/LutLight2D). Its published page describes narrative and asymmetric chess layers with card/dice/direct roles; this is a page/source inspection, not a claim we played the game. Levi also cites Gwent from The Witcher as inspiration for a memorable game within the game. Use original art and rules; reference images are inspiration only. The Technonomicon source study records the smallest card-art seam: one original Three-authored Major Arcana subject scene, baked through the fixed low-resolution path, then placed in one shared readable card layout. Prove one card at native and enlarged presentation before authoring a set; this does not add Astro or a second art engine.

Record these connected possibilities without locking them into one design:

- A small occult chess-like game played with inn guests and visitors.
- Collectible chess pieces and tarot cards, possibly earned by winning matches.
- Tarot/card collection may grant or teach in-world magic and lead to unusual events.
- Major Arcana may cause high-level named people to appear; recruiting them should be difficult and require actual interaction/conditions rather than automatically adding them to the roster.
- Player-built shrines, eventual teleportation between consenting players' shrines, and cult-building around people, places, beliefs, and rituals.
- Cards and pieces could travel with caravans, connecting the tabletop game to hospitality, exploration, trade, and multiplayer visits.

Pacing should mix immediate colony decisions measured in seconds, visible jobs,
building/crafting and optional short chess matches measured in minutes, and
standing-order/offline projects over longer spans. Chess must let the player
return to the colony when needed; it is a connected guest/knowledge/card/spell/
recruiting activity, not a blocking wait screen or a separate simulation clock.

The links are a product hypothesis: chess → cards/pieces → magic/encounters → people/shrines/cults. We can choose one small playable connection later; no whole deck, cult simulator, chess engine, teleport backend, or rarity economy is requested now. The existing physical ownership, validated command, fixed-step outcome and storyteller-event responsibilities remain the intended foundation. Future cross-homeland travel must use the same identity/ownership transfer as caravans, with explicit destination access.
