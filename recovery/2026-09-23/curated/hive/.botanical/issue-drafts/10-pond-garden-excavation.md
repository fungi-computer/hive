# Zen garden and fishing pond from excavated terrain

Status: future first terrain/water experience.

Explore ground/Minecraft-like excavation and moving water to build a small Zen garden and fishing pond. Avoid manual source-block or bucket puzzles: use a finite conserved amount, gravity, connected-surface leveling, and a pond fill-level order that creates hauling work, with later inlet, drain, and pump options. A candidate is active wet-cell simulation with sleeping settled regions and actual cross-Z openings; explicit sources and sinks own all additions/removals, and unloaded boundaries never silently discard or duplicate water. Pressure/connected vessels, stable levels, chunk crossing, and offline accounting require proof. Start with a dug basin, controlled inlet/outlet, explicit levels, and conservation across digging, filling, draining, save/reload, and chunk boundaries. The simple CA discussion at [W-shadow](https://w-shadow.com/blog/2009/09/01/simple-fluid-simulation/) and [Red Blob Games terrain from noise](https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/terrain-from-noise/) are references, not selected or copied implementations. This issue does not authorize a generic fluid engine, full ecology, CFD, ocean simulation, or shipped claim beyond the bounded garden/pond slice.

First useful proof: divert a small stream into a dug pond, control its flow and level, breach/drain it, and show consistent levels and accounted water after pause, save/reload, and return from an adjacent chunk. A spring or inlet has an explicit supply rate; conservation includes those additions and measured outflows. A later proof adds the fishing interaction and Zen garden presentation at intended scale. Plant cultivation can begin independently; pond moisture can feed it once this water behavior is proved.
