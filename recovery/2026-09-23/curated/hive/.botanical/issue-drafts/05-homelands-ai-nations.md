# Persistent homelands, offline helpers, and nations

Status: future direction after local caravan continuity.

First prove two clients issuing authorized commands against one authoritative same-homeland world. Then support a person's caravan visiting a partner's persistent homeland with the same people, possessions, books, and learned spells, followed by bounded offline AI helpers for declared work and eventually interacting nations and AI-managed settlements. On departure, settle or release source-world claims; never transfer stale material claims. Offline progress, timers, storyteller input, and multiplayer authority must remain separate concerns; SSE timing is never the simulation clock.

All clients share one authoritative world clock. Local fast-forward cannot let one
visitor advance a shared homeland independently; shared pause/speed policy is a
later multiplayer decision. Offline projects resume from durable world state in
bounded work, never from artificial wait timers or a client's frame clock. The
current browser speed control remains a local-prototype convenience until that
multiplayer slice is designed.

Levi accepts dependable ordinary guard/retreat/shelter standing orders and basic
offline protection for everyone. Future paid AI stewardship may add richer
situational planning, custom instructions, diplomacy and alerts, including
reactions to capture threats; it must not make basic protection from offline
kidnapping pay-only. AI receives the same information entitlements, permissions,
actions and world time as human control. Failure or exhausted paid budget leaves
ordinary character orders functioning. Actual pricing and practical-advantage
tuning remain unresolved economy decisions. No billing, model service or backend
is authorized here.

First useful proof: two local clients command different people in one homeland with authoritative duplicate-command handling. A later proof transfers one person and one physical item between homelands, then runs a bounded helper assignment and reconciles ownership without duplication. This is a local proof target, not authorization for backend deployment, paid services, or live Shiitake integration.
