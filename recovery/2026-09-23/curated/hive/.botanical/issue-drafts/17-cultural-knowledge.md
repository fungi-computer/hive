# Future direction: cultural knowledge transfer and controller authority

Published as https://github.com/fungi-computer/hive/issues/17 on 2026-09-07.

Humans are reserved for player-directed characters or characters under
player-delegated AI stewardship. World-directed populations use other peoples.
Keep controller authority separate from species, culture, faction and knowledge.

Elven cultures are nature-oriented knowledge sources and dwarven cultures are
machine-oriented knowledge sources. Record learned practices and their provenance
through concrete teaching, trade, observation or durable media; do not model a
global racial unlock. Start later with one practice transferred between two
identified characters. No culture simulation, AI service, species artwork,
backend or knowledge system enters the current home release.
