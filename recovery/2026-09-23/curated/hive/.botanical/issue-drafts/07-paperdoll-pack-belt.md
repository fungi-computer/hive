# Paper-doll gear, rotatable packs, and hauling arms

Status: future gameplay slice after home continuity.

Add a paper-doll equipment view with compatible helmet, armor, gloves, and boots slots; a rotatable geometric backpack grid; and a distinct belt pouch. Worn gloves are equipment while hand cargo is activity occupancy. Keep equipped gear, packed contents, and visible hauling cargo as separate locations with one owner per physical item. A large log may occupy arms without occupying backpack cells; weight and fit remain separate constraints. Dropping or removing a filled bag moves the bag and contents together without overflow loss or duplication. The first gear can be received as existing cargo; it need not wait for a crafting system.

First useful proof: receive one pack, belt, helmet, armor, gloves, and boots, rotate items into the pack and belt, carry a log in the arms, drop/re-equip the filled pack, cancel a haul, and save/reload while checking conservation.
