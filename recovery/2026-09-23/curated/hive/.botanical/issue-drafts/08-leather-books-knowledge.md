# Leather processing and physical knowledge books

Status: future authored crafting slice.

Start with primitive leather preparation/drying and a later refined tanning method. A batch owns its hide, reagent, work, cure, and output state across interruption; completion produces once. A physical booklet is movable cargo that can be lent, lost, copied, or carried by a caravan, while learned technique stays with the person who studies it. Keep the first chain authored and small; a real tanning recipe is optional for the initial proof, and libcolony still assigns ready work.

First useful proof: one person carries a tanning booklet and finite hides to another, the recipient studies it, prepares reagent, starts a batch, returns to ordinary work while it cures, and finishes a refined leather pack whose visible benefit differs from a crude pack. Removing the booklet stops an unfinished reading assignment without erasing learned knowledge.
